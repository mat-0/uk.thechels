> Twitch have recently opted-in every streamer into Amazon's generative AI training. You have to explicitly opt-out manually.

Twitch now uses streamer content — VODs, clips, stream chats, and channel text/images — to train Amazon's generative AI models. Opting out will stop any future use of your stream for AI use, but it is not possible to tell if previous content was used. Viewing someone else's stream and therefore that streamer's chat is governed/covered by their settings.

## How to opt out of AI training on Twitch

- Click on your profile icon
- Click `Settings` from the menu
- Choose the `Security and Privacy` tab
- The item third bottom is 'Training for Generative AI'
- Click the toggle to disable

## FAQ

### Is Twitch AI training opt-in or opt-out?

- Opt-out. Every account is enrolled by default as of August 2026.

### Where is the Twitch generative AI training setting?

- Settings → Security and Privacy → Training for Generative AI.

## Does opting out stop all AI use on Twitch?

- No. It only stops generative AI model training. AutoMod, captions, and other AI-driven features are unaffected.

## Does this apply retroactively?

- Unclear. Twitch has not said whether Amazon already trained models on content from before the opt-out was introduced.
